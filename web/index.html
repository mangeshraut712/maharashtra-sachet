Maharashtra Civic Alert Relay

Unofficial public-feed relay · not NDMA / MSDMA / IMD

This is not a government website. It only republishes public Common Alerting Protocol and allied feeds. It cannot send Wireless Emergency Alerts or cell broadcasts. For life-threatening events call 112.

Polygons come from NDMA SACHET CAP when the feed includes them. Light rain nowcasts often have no polygon.
