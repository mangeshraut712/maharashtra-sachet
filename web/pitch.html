Safety is part of the product

Clear provenance. Honest uncertainty.

Independent civic relay

Not a government site or emergency dispatch service. No cell broadcast, Wireless Emergency Alerts, or IPAWS origination.

Empty ≠ all-clear

An empty, stale, disabled, or unavailable feed cannot establish safety. In immediate danger, call 112.

Sample data stays sample data

The DEMO banner follows the walkthrough. Fixture notices do not describe a real incident or Metro disruption.

Limited coverage and AI

Exactly 36 Maharashtra districts; Goa excluded. District matches are not station warnings. Gemini explanations may be wrong: verify the original source.

Optional Jev shadow, situational layers, and CPCB remain off by default. They are not prerequisites for this demo.
